The problem

Exporters refund buyers for damage they never get to see.

  • 1The buyer is overseas, so all the exporter has is a photo.
  • 2Generative AI has made convincing damage photos easy to fake.
  • 3Each false claim costs the refund plus international freight for a replacement.

Most fraud tools look at payments, not damage claims. Detection can flag a fake photo, but whatever slips past still comes out of the exporter's pocket. They need insurance for what detection misses.

How Akita works

Our agent looks at each claim before the money moves.

When an exporter files a claim, Akita handles it from start to finish.

Who we serve

We start with furniture exporters selling into the U.S.

Why furniture

Large, expensive items, costly to ship twice. Damage claims, refund fraud and replacement shipments hit these businesses hard, and existing insurance leaves most of it uncovered.

Who buys

Owners, CEOs and operations leads, the people who absorb the losses themselves. We reach them directly through our founding team's 30-year furniture export network.

Where next

Other high-value export categories, and then businesses in any industry facing the new kinds of fraud that cheap generative AI makes possible.
