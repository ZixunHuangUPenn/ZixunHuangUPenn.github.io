01 — The problem

Exporters refund overseas buyers
based on photos they cannot verify.

STEP 1

Buyer sends a photo

"Arrived damaged." Thousands of miles away, no inspector on site.

STEP 2

GenAI makes it trivial

Realistic fake damage can now be generated in seconds.

STEP 3

Exporter pays anyway

Refusing risks the relationship, so the refund goes out.

THE COST

Refund + freight

Each false claim costs the refund plus international shipping of a replacement.

Fraud tools check payments, not claims. Detection can flag fake photos — but exporters still absorb every loss that gets through. They need insurance to cover what detection misses.

02 — What we're building

One agent loop. Every claim,
end to end.

  1. Gather evidencePulls shipping, inspection, photo and claims records automatically.
  2. DiagnoseRuns a library of specialized diagnostic and fraud-check skills.
  3. Decide & logApprove, escalate, or deny — every decision logged with its full reasoning chain.
